# EduHive (Eduvi)

## Purpose
Collaborative learning platform: live classrooms, courses, AI tutoring, whiteboard, polls, study notes, progress tracking, and gamified leaderboards.

## Users
- **Students** — learn, compete, track progress
- **Teachers** — create courses, run live sessions
- **Admins** — approvals, users, analytics

## Mode
Primarily **Operate** (task-focused product UI). Landing is **Persuade**.

## Brand voice
Clear, capable, focused. No hype. Hive metaphor = community + industrious learning.

## Anti-references
Purple/indigo AI gradients, glassmorphism everywhere, identical rounded feature cards, Inter/Roboto defaults, cream+terracotta craft palette, neon glows, floating blobs.
